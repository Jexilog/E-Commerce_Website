The Best In-Ear Monitors for Working Out

Posted on Apr. 28, 2025 by Soundstage Staff

When it comes to your workouts, the right playlist can make all the difference -- but so can the right gear.Ever experienced your earbuds fall out mid-sprint? Or found yourself fiddling with uncomfortable headphones during a heavy lift? You know it well how important it is to have a reliable, comfortable, and good sounding IEM’s that will accompany you through your workout.

So here’s a breakdown of the best in-ear monitors that we rightfully picked and recommend.

Moondrop Chu/Chu II

Why We Love It:
Budget-friendly without sacrificing sound
Beautiful, balanced audio
Lightweight, comfortable design


If you want high-quality sound without breaking the bank, the Moondrop  Chu/Chu II is a solid pick. The main reason this is the first to our list is because it’s small design that will fit any ear easily.

Though not specifically marketed for workouts, many users find them comfortable and durable enough for light to moderate exercise, especially if you swap to foam tips for extra security.

Best for: Casual workouts, light runs, indoor cycling.

Sony WF-1000XM5 (True Wireless)

Why We Love It:
Industry-leading noise cancellation
Amazing sound quality
Comfortable, lightweight build


For those who hate dealing with cords, the Sony WF-1000XM5 offers premium sound with the freedom of wireless. Sweat-resistant and designed for all-day comfort, they are perfect for everything from long runs to yoga classes.

Best for: Cardio, running, all-around fitness.
